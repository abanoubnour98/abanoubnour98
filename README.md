## About Me

I'm an **Information Systems graduate** interested in building practical software, improving digital processes, and solving business problems with technology.

My work and projects sit across **web development, technical implementation, AI-assisted workflows, automation, and digital growth**. I enjoy taking an idea from requirements and workflow design through implementation, testing, and deployment.

I also bring a professional background in digital marketing and affiliate partnerships. That experience helps me understand the commercial side of digital products: how people discover them, how websites convert visitors, how tracking works, and how technology supports acquisition and operations.

I use AI tools to accelerate research, development, and repetitive work, while focusing on understanding the requirements, reviewing the output, testing the result, and making sure the solution is useful.

- **Based in:** Cairo, Egypt

## How I Approach Building

1. **Understand the problem** — clarify the user, business objective, constraints, and expected outcome.
2. **Plan the solution** — define requirements, workflows, architecture, and practical milestones.
3. **Build in stages** — implement the smallest useful solution and keep the work understandable.
4. **Test and review** — check functionality, edge cases, usability, security, and maintainability.
5. **Deploy and improve** — document the result, monitor issues, and iterate based on feedback.
